    Privacy & Responsible AI Architecture

    • Zero Credential Storage: ScamRadar never stores or transmits bank passwords, OTPs, PINs, or financial credentials.
    • Approximate Coordinates: Geocoded coordinates are mapped to general neighborhood centroids with random privacy jitter. Exact residential addresses are forbidden.
    • Honest Local AI: Analysis uses a deterministic, rule-based NLP engine with weighted pattern scoring. We do not falsely claim to use black-box proprietary LLMs.
    • Responsible Phrasing: Reports are described as "reported patterns" and "potential risks," never definitive proof of criminal guilt.
    • Synthetic Seeding: Demo logs are explicitly labeled: “Synthetic demo data — not real-world incident reports.”
